How the word trail works

Move your mouse across the homepage and words from the archive briefly appear beside the pointer, drift upward, and fade.

Where the words come from

The trail draws at random from a small collection of words in the saved common-word cloud and short phrases from published posts. The random selection gives each item an equal chance after excluding the previous item.

How to control it

The trail starts on when you load the homepage with a mouse or trackpad and your device allows motion. Select Word Trail On or press Esc to stop it. Select Word Trail Off to restart it. The site does not save the setting between page loads.

The trail respects your device’s reduced-motion preference. It is decorative, so it does not intercept clicks or interrupt screen-reader announcements.
