Archive coverage and credits

Snapshot: November 5, 2026. Agent-assisted record review under Ian G. Williams’s direction. A reviewed record does not necessarily have a confirmed writer.

423 published Tagging the Tower records. The filter excludes 9 category drafts. The other ordinary posts listed below lack the Tagging the Tower category; revisions, attachments and other item types are not additional articles. This establishes completeness within the supplied export, not completeness of the historical website.

How credits were checked

  • 95 published records have source-supported credits: accepted bylines, metadata, guide rows, source biographies or explicit named article references were checked. This is not personal author confirmation.
  • 122 have declared credits only: a name appears in source metadata or author taxonomy, without a separately established byline.
  • 206 remain unresolved account credits: the posting account is retained for traceability, but is not established as the historical writer.

All article rows, credit entries and reading copies in this release use published posts. Curators, cited people, workshop leaders, image makers and earlier-version contributors are not automatically current article authors. Source-supported filters change the denominator; results must carry that qualification. Availability of bylines varies by year and article format, so this narrower subset is not a representative replacement for the whole archive.

Coverage across analyses

All 423 published records appear in the model manifest, with 301 included and 122 excluded for recorded reasons. The body-signal and thematic tables cover all 327 published local records; the earlier motif analysis uses 309 after its separate exclusions. The interpretation uses 21 selected sources. When credits change, the site updates the author summaries. It continues to use the same saved corpus and topic weights.
