Accessibility and known limitations

Working statement, November 2, 2026. The interface uses native buttons, labels and disclosure controls; tab selection has a visible state; charts offer selectable details; the publication network has an equivalent table. Dense charts may scroll horizontally. Responsive layouts and reduced-motion preferences are supported.

These implementation provisions do not constitute WCAG conformance or assistive-technology certification. A full screen-reader, keyboard-only and low-vision audit with actual participants has not been completed. Older standalone reports and source media may have different limitations. The text reader omits images, embedded media and formatting. Discovered barriers and the scope of browser checks are recorded in the work log.

Report a barrier to Ian G. Williams, identifying the page or view, action, device/browser, assistive technology if relevant, and expected behavior. No response-time commitment has been specified.
