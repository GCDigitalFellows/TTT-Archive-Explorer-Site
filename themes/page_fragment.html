Definitions, attribution and limits

Both keyword layers use eight saved dictionaries from the original audit. The broader layer searches title, tags and body together; it includes welcome posts and may match program boilerplate, negation or incidental mentions. Neither layer has been independently human-coded as a thematic reading. Curated/source-linked records and drafts are excluded from these charts.

The essay layer groups 19 purposively selected examples into six agent-developed interpretations. Unselected posts are not treated as negative examples. It uses counts only and cannot estimate archive-wide theme prevalence. Missing years in that sample remain missing. Post authors receive source-writing credit, not endorsement of these assignments.

Credit filtering changes the denominator. “Provisional” means a posting account supplies the attribution, not an independently resolved byline. Identity grouping preserves the original credit. Publication year does not prove membership in a Fellows cohort.
