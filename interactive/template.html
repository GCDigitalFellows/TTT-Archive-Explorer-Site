How to read one value

A topic model finds recurring groups of words across texts. MALLET is the software used here; a topic’s descriptive label is an interpretation, not a definitive category. NLTK supplies word-counting and language-processing tools.

In the primary eight-topic model, the 2012–2013 cell for “Research support & community programming” is 46.8%: the mean weight of that word grouping across 35 modeled posts, giving each post equal weight. This does not mean 46.8% of posts are exclusively about community programming. Changing the model or averaging setting changes the quantity displayed.

A seed initializes the statistical procedure; comparing seeds tests whether patterns depend on that starting choice. A cohort here should be read cautiously: the available signed lists identify active teams in particular semesters, not complete entering classes.

Seed sensitivity · eight-topic models

Jensen–Shannon distance: 0 means identical word distributions, 1 maximally different. Topics are matched by minimum total distance; their original numbers do not align automatically.

Why this style? Sources and methodology

· representative visual direction

    We cross-referenced dated design-system milestones with contemporary reporting, implementation examples, and clearly marked forecasts. We coded their visible features, then translated those features into a readable research interface. This is a curated design history, not a measurement of which style won each year.

    Colors, local font substitutes and decorative accents are our adaptations. Controls, contrast, chart meaning and reduced-motion preferences take priority over historical imitation. Research is bounded to October 31, 2026; the 2026 preset is forecast-led.

    How to read and share this explorer

    All interactions use saved analysis outputs. No model is retrained and no data is sent to a service. You can share this one HTML file and open it in a browser without a server or additional software.

    Topic labels for the eight-topic, seed-42 run are agent-developed interpretive labels; independent human confirmation is not documented. Other runs show their leading words. The same topic number can mean something different in a different run.

    The readable text, full cleaning ledger, stoplists, and source code remain in the analysis bundle. Consult the reviewer guide for extraction limits, incomplete cohort evidence, and the distinction between writers and posting accounts.

    Original project work: MIT. Source posts and media retain separate terms: source rights · Creative Commons acknowledgments. Descriptive exploration of the supplied WordPress export.
