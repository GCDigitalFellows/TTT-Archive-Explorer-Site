Historical Fellows and other GCDI roles

The GCDI People page supplies dated alumni role histories. These records add institutional context to the archive; membership does not establish who wrote a post.

Academic-calendar interpretation, confirmed by Ian G. Williams: a range such as 2012–2015 covers 2012–2013, 2013–2014 and 2014–2015. These are role intervals, rather than exact appointment dates or complete semester rosters. A fall-only entry stays fall-only. Summer matches are not inferred.

The archive also includes guest-authored posts by students who were not Fellows at publication, including participants in the Graduate Center Digital Research Institute (GCDRI). Ian supplied this contextual clarification. Guest authorship is an authorship relationship; participation and fellowship appointments are separate roles. Assess fellowship status at the post date, rather than treating it as a lifetime identity. A missing directory match alone establishes neither guest status nor nonmembership. Participant reflections remain in the full archive and its applicable analytical populations; a Fellow-only filter intentionally selects a narrower population.

What changes in the archive analyses?

The topic and word views can compare the original signed lists, reviewed name resolutions, dated Digital Fellow intervals, any dated GCDI role, and the union of resolved signed teams with dated Digital Fellow intervals. A match concerns a recorded writer credit; account-only authorship remains unresolved. The source-supported authorship filter is a separate restriction.

The original signed-list CSV and its saved topic means are preserved. New summaries re-average the same fitted post weights; no NLTK cleaning or MALLET fitting was rerun. Neither an appointment interval nor a lack of a directory match settles article authorship.
