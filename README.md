## About the project

[Tagging The Tower](https://gcdi.commons.gc.cuny.edu/category/news/tagging-the-tower/) (TTT) is the official blog of the GC Digital Fellows, an interdisciplinary team of doctoral student fellows at the CUNY Graduate Center. Posting on TTT started in Fall 2012 alongside the inception of the fellowship program, and to date the blog has produced a growing collection of posts covering a wide range of topics, written by Digital Fellows and guest contributors (421 published records in the November 1, 2026 data snapshot used for this project). This project started from a question of what the collective wisdom of the blog might offer, as an archive of born-digital academic gray literature, and wondering what the collective totality of Digital Fellows have to say about what "digital scholarship" is - and can be. Born in an era of digital transformation that predates the current reorganization of higher education around generative AI technologies, the blog archive offers historical perspective and precedent for how graduate students navigate uncertainty, technological change, and the complicated processes of living through disruption - while often seeking to make creative, critical interventions in the university, and their disciplines, along the way.

**Current corpus, November 9, 2026:** the preserved November 1 snapshot plus two newly supplied posts contains **423 published records**, with **301 modeled posts**. Five main models and the thirty-fit sensitivity comparison were rerun; earlier results remain preserved. See [refresh provenance and checks](analysis/refresh/2026-11-09/README.md).

The project's first intended in-person use is at the [NYC PIT Pop Up](https://nycpitpopup.org/), hosted by the [CUNY Public Interest Technology Lab](https://pitlab.net), where GC Digital Fellows will organize an open house on the road event on November 12, 2026. Interested parties can register here: https://luma.com/9659b5tn

Using AI-assisted coding tools (primarily ChatGPT and Codex), the project seeks to render an ongoing analysis, while also making the archive legible and easier to navigate than its legacy WordPress format. It is a work-in-progress and will be periodically updated.

The remainder of this README.MD file is mostly updated and generated by AI tools, with some light human editing.

An archive and interpretation companion to the **Tagging the Tower** archive at the CUNY Graduate Center. Explore posts, recorded author credits, academic-year evidence, statistical topics and thematic signals, with source-linked interpretation and transparent methods.

The previous published interface is preserved privately in [the dated archive](archives/public-site/2026-11-04-before-welcoming-pathways/README.md), with a complete file manifest and rollback instructions.
