Three layers of credit

Source writing: the credited people whose posts and linked works form the archive. Project contributions: the documented human direction and critique that shaped this analysis. AI delegation: tasks carried out with the agent and its tools. Being named in the archive does not make someone a coauthor of this analysis, endorse the findings, or establish responsibility for the code.

The source register includes every human-like credited name in the current 430-record inventory, including local writers, named authors of curated source works, and draft metadata. Provisional account attributions remain labeled; name strings are not a verified census of distinct people.

From export to explorer

How the work was produced

  1. Define the question. Ian requested trends across academic-year cohorts, further text analysis, and ways to explore the results.
  2. Audit the export. The scripts retain 430 category records, distinguish 421 published posts from nine drafts, and separate local writing, curation, posting accounts and source authors.
  3. Establish evidence. Explicit opening bylines and signed contributions can override a provisional account credit. Mentions, photo credits, instructors and acknowledgments do not automatically establish article authorship. Same-semester cohort matches use roster evidence, not inferred membership.
  4. Prepare and model the corpus. The saved NLTK workflow selected and cleaned 299 modeled posts; five MALLET runs explored statistical word/topic mixtures. Corpus exclusions and cleaning choices are documented in the NLP report.
  5. Interpret and present. Agent-generated prose, interpretive topic labels from the earlier saved analysis, interactive charts, annual design lenses and the network were assembled for inspection. Topic labels are interpretations, not discoveries guaranteed by the model.
  6. Correct attribution. Ian flagged missing authors. The source-body review corrected eight posts, recovered ten additional local credited names, removed two inherited cohort matches, and recomputed attribution-dependent summaries. The saved corpus and fitted topic weights stayed fixed for that correction. Read the evidence and remaining uncertainty.
  7. Check and disclose. Automated numerical checks, literal-evidence tests and browser review inspect the implementation. The retained baseline and separate revision ledger distinguish unchanged results from intentional corrections. This page adds acknowledgment and process metadata without changing the fitted analysis.

The current source-credit register is built from the corrected inventory, rather than hand-copying a shortlist. The readable generator is build_credits.py; reviewed name mappings and the process profile are kept separately. Counts and source links can be regenerated and checked.
