A defensible qualitative statement

The selected archive posts portray digital scholarship as a collective practice involving learning, technical work, care and critical judgment. This is an AI-assisted interpretation of 19 purposively selected posts, not an evaluation of program effectiveness.

Coverage and reuse cautions

The archive spans 2012–2026; 2026–27 is partial. Signed-list evidence does not provide complete early membership or entering-cohort histories. The expanded essay includes 2018–2022 examples but is not a representative sample. The 161 grouped author entries are a separate, partly provisional register across local, source and draft credits. Do not report them as a verified total of Fellows or unique human participants.
